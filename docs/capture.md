# Capture: shortcuts, Mac recording, displays

Read this before changing `src/shortcut.rs`, `src/capture.rs`, `src/screens.rs` or the picker and capsule placement in `src/ui.rs`.

## Shortcuts (`src/shortcut.rs`, `is_capture_shortcut` in `src/capture.rs`)

A press of `⌥⇧S` (or the shortcut set in Settings), however long it's held, records the display under the pointer as soon as its modifiers come up, auto-cropped, with a brief red edge glow from the overlay as the start cue, and the capsule springs open onto switch chips (Area · Window · Screen · iPhone if cabled; `shortcut::Reveal` and its `REVEAL_*` constants) until 3.5 s pass or the user clicks, scrolls or draws (never while hovered). A chip (`switch_recording`) restarts rather than retargets: Window/Area discard the few seconds and morph the capsule into the picker, iPhone discards and starts the phone.

With the `rememberSelection` experiment the press records what was chosen last instead (`record_shortcut_target`, also the menu's Record item, which names it): every recording of a window, area, screen or iPhone is kept in `last-selection.json` beside the captures (`remember_selection`), and `capture::reuse_selection` takes the same window if it's still listed, else the front window of its app, an area while a display holds its centre, the iPhone once the helper finds one cabled (`record_iphone_if_cabled`), and the screen under the pointer otherwise, so a press always records at once. A recording that stands in for a choice that's gone (`standing_in`) keeps the choice and its banner says why. The chips light what's recorded (`Pres::Recording.mode`, `recording_mode`); Screen on a window or area restarts on the screen. A double press (`shortcut::Shortcut::double_press_chooses`: S again while ⌥⇧ is held, or a press within `DOUBLE_PRESS_MS` of the start) opens the picker on the mode last used (`choose_target`), dropping a recording the first press started.

With the `showTarget` experiment what's recorded is plain. A window recording first brings its window to the front with its app (`capture::bring_to_front`: `AXRaise` and `AXMain` on the `AXWindow` whose `_AXUIElementGetWindow` id it is, then `AXFrontmost`, off the main thread). The native capsule's banner (`native/pill/tip.rs` `Banner`, `NativePill::announce`, `recording_detail`) names it for 2.4 s at the top of the window or area, or below the capsule for a screen. A start from the shortcut glows along the window's or area's edges as along a screen's. And `focus_window` (the picker's highlighted window, or the recorded one) has the overlay darken and hatch the parts of it other app windows cover (`windows::covers_of`, re-read every 160 ms, at most 16), since a window recording shows the window there, not what's on top.

Recording starts on the release rather than the press so nothing is captured while ⌥⇧ is still down (⇧ is the ink key), and Esc before the release cancels. The picker opens only from the menu's **Choose What to Record…** (or a chip), on the pointer's display and on the mode last used; there the shortcut records what it's on, and repeats of a held S never record (`handle_selection_key`'s `repeat`). While recording, a press finishes, ignoring one within 400 ms of the start.

With the `shapeTools` experiment the native capsule shows the pen, line, arrow and box while recording (`ElemId::TOOLS`, `Pres::Recording.tools`), and `set_tool_hotkeys` registers the record shortcut's modifiers with 1–4 (`tool_modifiers`: ⌥⇧ when those are ⇧ alone or none) for that recording only; Carbon hands a registered hotkey to Scrawl alone, so no keys reach the app being shown and none are taken outside a recording. The chosen tool is `SharedCaptureTelemetry::tool` (each recording starts with the pen). A ⇧ stroke with a shape tool keeps its start (`shape_anchor`) and is redrawn on every poll as the whole shape to the pointer (`shapes::ends`, `shapes::outline`): ⌘ (Ctrl on Windows) snaps lines and arrows to 45° and boxes to squares, ⌥ grows the shape both ways from its start. While a shape is drawn those modifiers don't end the ink (`shaping`); with the pen they still do. Its points are the outline at most 3 pt apart, so the overlay, keyframes, crop and export draw it like any ink, and `Stroke.tool` makes it a mark of its own (`Shape::Line`, `Arrow`, `Box`).

Resting the pointer 0.5 s on a native capsule button shows its tooltip (`model::tip_of`: what it does and the keys that do the same, from `PillSnapshot.keys`); within 0.4 s of one hiding the next shows at once, and a press hides it until the pointer leaves that button. It's its own AppKit panel (`native/pill/tip.rs`) under the capsule, or above it near the screen's bottom, since AppKit's own tooltips never show while Scrawl isn't the active app. It stays ordered in at alpha 0 and is in `excluded_window_ids` (never recorded) and `passthrough_window_ids` (clicks under it are the app's).

Carbon hotkeys and the key event tap report presses through `emit_capture_shortcut` (deduplicated within 90 ms); while a press waits for its release the UI loop polls the modifiers and takes Esc from the key tap (`tick_shortcut`, `take_escape`), so the release is seen whichever window has focus. Esc from the key tap also closes an open picker when another app has the keys.

## Mac captures (`src/capture.rs`)

The sampler keeps only small candidates (480 px long edge, in a `CandidateBuffer` capped at 384 MB that thins evenly and halves its sampling rate when full, never dropping the start or click/stroke frames). At finish the chosen keyframes are decoded from `recording.mp4` at native size (2560 px Screen, 1280 px Window/Region, never above the display's own scale, so a 1x 1920 pt display records 1920 px), shifted by `videoOffsetMs` (when the movie started on the capture clock); if decoding fails the candidates are used. macOS prompts over a Screen or Area capture (`windows::system_prompts_over`: UserNotificationCenter, SecurityAgent; ScreenCaptureKit's "bypass the system private window picker" alert comes back every 30 days) are left out of the recording through the content filter when ScreenCaptureKit lists them, frames that may show one are dropped from the candidates (`CandidateBuffer::drop_since`), and clicks on them aren't recorded. `scrawl capture-sim` drives this headless for memory and timing checks.

## More than one display (`src/screens.rs`)

Every rect and point is global top-left points (`CGEvent`, `CGDisplayBounds`, `SCWindow` frames, `TargetFrame`); displays left of or above the primary one are negative. `screens::flip` converts AppKit frames, `screens::current()` lists displays with menu bar, notch and `backingScaleFactor` (main thread), `screens::displays()` the CoreGraphics view (any thread).

A capture belongs to one display: a tap takes the one under the pointer, Window the one holding the window's centre, Area the one holding its centre (clamped to it; `sourceRect` is display-local via `screens::source_rect`); `TargetFrame.scale` is that display's.

The picker opens on the pointer's display and follows the pointer between displays (window, Metal overlay and capsule: `follow_pointer_display`) except while an area is dragged or iPhone is shown; the overlay `move_to`s the target's display when recording starts and follows the iPhone mirror if it's dragged. The capsule (`NativePill::place_on`) sits on the display in use, `PANEL_Y` below its top or lower for a taller menu bar or the notch (`screens::pill_origin`), or where it was dragged to: a press on it that moves 4 pt drags it (no click), and the drop is kept as an offset from that home, for every capture and display (saved in `capsule-offset` beside the captures, so a restart keeps it), while the capsule is kept inside the display's visible frame whatever it grows to (`native/pill/place.rs`). A drop within 24 pt of home goes home. Test with a `CGVirtualDisplay` (see `scripts/virtual-display.swift`), never on Kit's desk Mac.
